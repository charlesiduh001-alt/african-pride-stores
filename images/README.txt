number printed on it. So Active, Active Gold and Laziz Premium each share one
photo across their sizes. That is accurate, not a shortcut.

A size with no photo of its own simply shows the product's default photo, so
missing ones never look broken. To add one, save it here and add a line to the
images block of that product in script.js.

Tips for good product shots:
  - Square, around 800 by 800 pixels.
  - Plain white or very light background.
  - The whole product in frame with a little space around it.
  - Save as .jpg and keep each file under about 300kb so the site stays fast.
